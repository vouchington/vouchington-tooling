Retrospective journal audit: one journal entry may hold several CI-failure or sandbox blocks, and blocks that name an audit type but do not match the grammar are reported as an `unparsed` block instead of leaving the section at `none observed`. Codex child transcripts that inherited no token totals segment with a zero baseline, and a segmentation failure names its reason. `compose.facts.pr` accepts a positive integer as well as a digit string.
